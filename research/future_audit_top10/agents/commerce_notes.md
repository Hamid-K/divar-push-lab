# Commerce, video, and banking app version inventory

**Scope:** Digikala (`com.digikala` and separately `com.digikala.diagon`), Aparat (`com.aparat`), and Baam (`ir.bmi.bam.nativeweb`). The audit window is **2023-01-01 through 2026-10-05**. This is an acquisition lead list, not an assertion that every released build has been found or that any build was audited. The companion CSV contains **113 package-matched observations**: 58 dated observations within the window, 51 directly observed pre-2023 baselines, and 4 undated leads whose year cannot be established. The 113 observations represent 96 distinct published version labels across the three brands. Repeated labels remain separate when a source lists more than one date or store entry, because APK identity has not been established.

## Evidence and date semantics

- [Digikala Uptodown version history](https://digikala.en.uptodown.com/android/versions) supplied dated 2022–2026 entries for `com.digikala`, including `-CB`, `-MK`, and `-HW` suffixes. [APKCombo old versions](https://apkcombo.com/digikala/com.digikala/old-versions/) supplied additional 2015–2019 baselines. [Café Bazaar's current page](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/com.digikala) independently exposes `3.2.3-CB` and a last-update date of 5 Mordad 1405 (2026-07-27). A [separate Uptodown history](https://digikala-diagon.en.uptodown.com/android/versions) identifies `com.digikala.diagon` and has 20 dated `-GP` observations; its [2.9.4-GP file page](https://digikala-diagon.en.uptodown.com/android/download) prints one SHA-256. These are two distinct Android package IDs under the Digikala name. Their developer/signing relationship has not been verified from APK bytes. Suffixes are preserved as published; their distribution meaning is not assumed.
- [Aparat Uptodown version history](https://aparat.en.uptodown.com/android/versions), an [older Uptodown page](https://aparat.it.uptodown.com/android/download), and [APKCombo old versions](https://apkcombo.com/aparat/com.aparat/old-versions/) supplied dated labels. [Café Bazaar's current page](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/com.aparat) exposes `7.2.1` and 28 Shahrivar 1405 (2026-09-19). This duplicates an Uptodown version/date observation but is a different store listing, so exact byte equivalence remains open.
- [Baam Café Bazaar](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/ir.bmi.bam.nativeweb) and [Myket](https://myket.ir/app/ir.bmi.bam.nativeweb) show the current `4.26.1.635` version. Package-and-version matched historical records came from [Certfa's archived Bazaar scans](https://certfa.com/lab/android-app/sha1/ff1e80a620438a4ee13e31ef2e284de5bf41534b/), [APKCombo](https://apkcombo.com/ar/%D9%87%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%87-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D9%85%D9%84%DB%8C/ir.bmi.bam.nativeweb/), [PGYER](https://www.pgyer.com/apk/fa/apk/ir.bmi.bam.nativeweb/download), and [Barato](https://barato.ir/bam-android/). APKCombo labels its uploader **APKFlash Uploader**, which is not proof of bank provenance. Barato currently labels `4.25.3.632` but prints two conflicting update dates, so that row is undated.
- `archive_file_date`, `store_last_update_date`, `third_party_archive_last_update_date`, and `APKCombo_update_date` mean **the site's published date**, not a verified original release date. Historical Certfa dates reflect the Bazaar last-update field in its scan snapshot. Persian dates were converted to Gregorian for sorting. Undated version labels are excluded from the dated audit-window count.
- `artifact_type` describes what the *page* offers or records. **No APK/XAPK bytes were downloaded, hashed, signature-checked, or analyzed.** Three Baam SHA-256 values come from VirusTotal links on Certfa pages and one Digikala `com.digikala.diagon` SHA-256 is printed by Uptodown. They are page-linked/page-published identifiers, not verified local hashes. Blank hashes and version codes are intentionally blank; codes from another archive were not copied onto potentially different builds.

## Important gaps and follow-up acquisition

1. **Digikala:** the `com.digikala` Uptodown list is sparse in 2023–2025, and APKCombo jumps from 2019 to much newer labels. The separate `com.digikala.diagon` archive has `-GP` builds through `2.9.4-GP`, plus older-numbered files given late 2024/2025 archive dates; those dates must not be treated as a developer release sequence. Neither source supplies a complete store release history. Acquire both packages separately, preserve all suffixes, and record manifest code, signing certificate, hash, and exact archive URL. The current `3.2.3-CB` and listed `3.2.3-MK` must remain separate pending byte comparison.
2. **Aparat:** the list has a large 2023–2025 gap; only one `5.3.1` 2024 archive entry bridges it. Uptodown lists several repeated version labels on different dates. Those could be variants or reuploads; no hash is published in the list. Download and hash before deduplication. A [bulk mirror listing](https://downloadapkforandroid.com/apps/aparat/) displays many versions but gives them the same 2026-03-17 site date; that is not evidence that those versions released then.
3. **Baam:** package-matched evidence is especially thin before late 2023 and between October 2024 and September 2026. Certfa exposes historical package/version pairs and three linked VT SHA-256 values, but its scanner verdicts were **not** adopted as security conclusions. Prioritize direct bank/Bazaar/Myket APK acquisitions and signing-chain comparison before trusting mirrors. PGYER's `N2.6.7`, `3.2.8.10`, and `4.7.2.269` old-version links have no version-specific date.
4. **Identity-only Baam leads kept out of the CSV:** the [bank press report](https://khabarfarsi.com/u/156362076) names `4.6.1.244` on 2023-11-04; the [IranApp article](https://iranapp.me/bam/) names `4.8.1.277` on 2024-01-28; [bank Telegram](https://t.me/s/bankmelli_ir/7906) mentions `4.9.1` and `4.10.1`; another [bank post](https://t.me/s/bankmelli_ir?before=9455) mentions `4.23.1`; [fan posts](https://telegram.me/s/balefestival?before=1190) mention `4.2.2.190`, `4.2.2.191`, `4.3.1.206`, and [another fan post](https://t.me/s/Balefestival/1194) mentions `4.4.1.218`. A [Soft98 article](https://soft98.org/%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AF-%D9%86%D8%B1%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%87%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%87-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%86%DA%A9-%D9%85%D9%84%DB%8C/?amp=1) also lists `4.2.2.191`. These pages do not pair each version with an inspectable `ir.bmi.bam.nativeweb` APK, so they are research leads rather than package-matched inventory rows. Barato previously appeared in search results as `4.25.2.631`, but its live page now says `4.25.3.632`; only the live package-matched entry is inventoried.

**Acquisition gate:** a version label alone is not an artifact. Before a future audit claims coverage, save the exact APK or XAPK, manifest package and version code, SHA-256, signer certificate fingerprint, source URL, retrieval time, and any split APK set. Compare same-version entries by hash before collapsing them.
